Overview

EduSense is an Android/iOS app that turns your phone into an AI-powered slide remote. Using computer-vision gesture recognition, you can advance, go back, or point to slides with simple hand movements—no extra hardware required.

Key Features

Works with PowerPoint, Google Slides, Keynote and any web deck. Connects via Wi-Fi; zero-config on the same LAN.

FAQ

Does EduSense work offline?

Yes. Gestures are processed on-device; no internet required after installation.

Which presentation apps are supported?

Any desktop app that accepts keyboard shortcuts (PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides in browser, etc.).
